# Copyright safety

- The interface, icon and decorative elements are original and code-drawn.
- Do not add competitor logos, copied templates, unlicensed photographs, characters, celebrity images, lyrics, poems or branded artwork.
- Write original guidance in plain language and never attribute original wording to a real person.
- Verify licences and retain evidence before adding any third-party asset.
- Customer-entered content remains the customer's responsibility under the terms of service.
