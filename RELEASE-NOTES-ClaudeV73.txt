InvoiceGeneratorNow ClaudeV73

The document controls now read as the starting point.


Why
- The two boxes that decide WHAT you are making, the Document and Language
  card and the Currency, logo and options panel, sat in exactly the same plain
  white card as every other panel on the page. Nothing marked them as the
  first decision, so the eye had no reason to land there before scrolling on.

What changed
- Both boxes now carry the accent: a tinted field (--accent-soft), a defined
  1.5px accent border, and selects outlined in the accent on a white
  background. Labels lift to the brand navy at weight 600.
- The selects deliberately stay white inside the tint. The tint frames the
  control; the value you have chosen stays the highest-contrast thing in the
  box rather than competing with its own background.
- The disclosure marker on Currency, logo and options picks up the accent, and
  the helper line under the language select stays quiet against the tint.

On the thick blue line
- A flat 3px blue box was the obvious reading of the request, and it is the
  one that would have looked cheap. Weight alone reads as a warning state.
  What makes this feel premium is the tint doing the work of separation, with
  the border defining the edge at a restrained 1.5px.

Both themes
- Dark mode was checked first, because a light-blue tint is exactly the kind
  of treatment that breaks there. It does not: --accent-soft is a deep navy
  (#1a2740) in dark and --accent lifts to #5b83f0, so the same rules produce a
  correctly weighted dark panel rather than a pale box.
- Measured:
      light  label  #12325e on #eef3ff   11.50:1
      light  border #2a56d6 on #eef3ff    5.56:1
      dark   label  #dbe6fb on #1a2740   11.88:1
      dark   border #5b83f0 on #1a2740    4.23:1
  Text clears WCAG AAA in both themes. Borders clear 1.4.11 for non-text UI
  components, which needs 3:1, in both.

Cascade
- The block sits last in the stylesheet so it beats the generic .field-card
  rules and the earlier dark-theme overrides on ordering and specificity,
  rather than forcing it with !important.
- Verified by resolving each property to its winning declaration:
      .field-card.doc-controls background  -> var(--accent-soft)
      .field-card.doc-controls border      -> 1.5px solid var(--accent)
      > .doc-options background            -> var(--accent-soft)
  and the same in dark.

Unchanged
- Styling only. No markup, no JavaScript, no behaviour.
- The print stylesheet already hides both boxes, so nothing reaches a printed
  or downloaded document.
- Verified intact: V72 receipt payment date, V71 preset removal (3 of 3 files
  clean), V69 document layouts (7 custom), V68 article fix, V67 deep-link fix,
  V66 date-overlay guards (4 of 4).
