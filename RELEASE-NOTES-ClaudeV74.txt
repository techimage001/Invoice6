The header Sign up button: two separate faults, both real.


Fault 1: it vanished under the cursor
- Hovering the button turned it invisible rather than highlighting it.
- Cause is a specificity collision. The header rule
      .top nav a:hover { color:var(--ink); background:var(--bg-2) }
  scores (0,2,2), while the button rule
      .btn:hover { background:var(--accent-strong) }
  scores (0,2,0). The header rule wins, so on hover the button lost its accent
  fill and took var(--bg-2), a near-white panel.
- At the same time .btn keeps colour:var(--accent-ink) forced with !important,
  so the text stayed white. White on #eef2f9 measures 1.12 to 1. The button was
  still there and still clickable; it simply could not be seen.
- Fixed by restating the button's own colours at a specificity that wins,
      .top nav a.btn:hover  (0,3,2)
  rather than by weakening the nav rule that every other header link relies on.
- Measured: hover contrast goes from 1.12 to 1 up to 8.07 to 1.

Fault 2: it did not do anything
- The button linked to /#invgen, which only scrolls to the builder. No signup
  form exists there.
- register.php and login.php both turned out to be 302 redirects back to that
  same anchor, so there was no signup page anywhere on the site.
- The only signup form in the entire application sits inside the gate panel,
  and that panel appeared solely when someone hit the free-document limit.
  Anyone who wanted to verify their email BEFORE running out had no route to
  do it, and the header invited them to try.
- The button now opens that same panel on demand. Same form, same
  /api/subscribe.php, same verification link, same /api/status.php unlock. No
  new page, no new endpoint, no change to the email flow itself.
- The email field takes focus when the panel opens.

The copy problem this created, and its fix
- The panel's wording is written for the limit case: "You have used your free
  documents on this browser." Shown to someone who has used none, that is
  simply untrue.
- Opening it from the header now shows "Verify your email to unlock unlimited
  use" with matching body copy.
- More importantly, showGate() now RESTORES the limit wording every time it
  opens. Without that, once a visitor had opened the panel voluntarily, later
  hitting the real limit would have kept showing the voluntary copy and never
  told them they had run out.
- Verified both directions: voluntary open shows the unlock copy; a subsequent
  limit-triggered open reverts to the limit copy.
