Ten new document types and four synonym pages. 26 documents to 36, 26 landing

Why these, and why not more invoice pages
- Search Console showed the site ranking positions 8 to 11 for waybill,
  return note and balance letter inside two weeks with no backlinks, while
  progress invoice sat at 26 to 47. The pattern is clear: you win on the niche
  documents the large invoicing sites do not bother with, and struggle where
  they compete.
- A correction to my earlier reading: zero clicks on 55 impressions is not a
  signal. At those positions the expected click count was 0.35, so zero is the
  normal outcome at that volume, not evidence of a title problem. The right
  conclusion was the opposite of the one I first drew: if pages rank on page
  one with no authority, more pages of the same shape should rank too.

TIER 1, the same niche shape as what already ranks
    delivery-challan     Description | Qty | Unit | Remarks
                         quantities not prices, plus origin, destination,
                         carrier and a receiver signature
    consignment-note     Description | Packages | Weight | Dimensions
    remittance-advice    Invoice date | Invoice number | Invoice amount |
                         Amount paid, so one payment can be allocated across
                         several invoices
    payment-voucher      Particulars | Account code | Amount, with prepared,
                         approved and received signatures
    cash-receipt         receipt layout with a separate payment-received date

TIER 2, recurring commercial need
    rent-invoice         invoice layout with a rental period field
    rent-receipt         receipt layout with period covered and payment date
    job-sheet            Date | Work carried out | Hours | Parts used,
                         no prices, customer signature on site
    retainer-invoice     invoice layout with the retained period
    refund-note          references the original invoice; the one genuine gap
                         in the Search Console data

TIER 3, synonyms rather than new builders
    progress-billing  -> builds a progress invoice
    quotation         -> builds a quote
    credit-memo       -> builds a credit note
    debit-memo        -> builds a debit note
- These are NOT duplicate pages. Each has its own keyword, title, first
  answer, five FAQs and body copy written for the synonym, and each explains
  the relationship to the canonical term rather than pretending to be a
  different document. A new 'builds' key in the registry points the call to
  action at the document type that actually produces the file, so
  credit-memo-template.php opens the credit note builder.
