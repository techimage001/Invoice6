InvoiceGeneratorNow ClaudeV59

Dark theme readability, and mobile wayfinding on the generator.


The dark theme, properly diagnosed
- The heading on every page was invisible in dark mode. Not low contrast,
  not faint: gone.
- The cause was a second :root block added by the V41 premium pass. It
  introduced a new set of tokens (--ink-1, --ink-3, --rule, --surface,
  --surface-2, --shadow-sm, --shadow-md) and rewrote h1, h2 and h3 to use
  --ink-1. But the [data-theme="dark"] block predates V41 and was never
  updated, so none of those seven tokens had a dark value.
- In dark mode --ink-1 therefore stayed at its light value of #0b1220, and
  the dark page background --bg is also #0b1220. The headings were being
  painted in exactly the same colour as the surface behind them. Measured
  contrast: 1.00 to 1.
- Five other things broke the same way. --rule stayed at #e8ecf3, so borders
  drew as near-white lines on a dark page. --surface stayed at #ffffff, which
  is why the Document and Document-language card rendered as a white box in
  an otherwise dark layout. --ink-3 left meta text below the contrast floor.

What changed
- All seven missing tokens now have dark values in the [data-theme="dark"]
  block, so they flip with the theme like every other token.
- Measured after the fix:
    headings   --ink-1 #f2f6fd on #0b1220   17.28 to 1   (WCAG AAA needs 7.0)
    meta text  --ink-3 #9aa8c4 on #0b1220    7.82 to 1   (WCAG AA  needs 4.5)
- Six rules that assumed a light surface now follow the token instead, so the
  form cards, the options panel and the trust band stop staying white.
- Verified: no colour token used anywhere in the stylesheet is now missing a
  dark-mode value.


Mobile: finding the document controls
- On a phone the Document type and Document language selectors looked like
  they did not exist. They did, but they were below the entire invoice
  preview, because the mobile layout sets the preview to order 1 and the whole
  form to order 2. On desktop the controls sit above the document, which is
  why it only looked broken on mobile.
- Per your decision the document stays first. There is now a full-width
  "Edit details" button directly under the preview that jumps straight to the
  controls, so it is one tap rather than a long scroll.
- The jump is a plain anchor with smooth scrolling and a scroll margin so the
  target does not land under the sticky header. It is disabled automatically
  for anyone who has asked for reduced motion. No JavaScript involved.
- The button is mobile only. Desktop already shows the controls above the
  document and does not need it.


Mobile: the sideways scroll
- The document is deliberately rendered at 660px on mobile rather than being
  shrunk to fit. That decision is unchanged, and it is the right one: the
  earlier approach scaled the text to roughly 9px and made the document
  impossible to edit on a phone.
- The problem was that nothing told you the document scrolled. A page cut off
  at the right edge with no cue just reads as broken.
- There is now a short hint pill above the document saying the document
  swipes sideways, and a soft fade on the right edge of the pane as a visual
  signal that there is more content that way. The fade is suppressed in full
  screen, where nothing is cut off.


Packaging
- Asset cache-buster bumped from v58 to v59. Without this the browser serves
  the cached stylesheet and none of the above appears.
